# bounded-review

Freeze the candidate before review. Review task scope, acceptance criteria, diff and deterministic checks. Findings must identify location, failure mode, impact and evidence class. Do not invent defects from missing context. Two normal review/fix loops maximum; after that escalate unresolved evidence. PASS requires no corroborated blocker/high finding and enough evidence for the risk level.
